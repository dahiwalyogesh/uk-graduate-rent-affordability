# LinkedIn Post

I wanted to answer a simple question: **where does a graduate salary go furthest on rent in the UK?**

I used Python to compare a £28,500 graduate salary with average room rents in 14 UK cities. After estimated 2025/26 Income Tax and National Insurance, that salary works out to about £2,003 a month in take-home pay.

The difference between cities is striking:

- **Inner London:** £1,002 a month, about 50% of take-home pay
- **Edinburgh, Bath and Oxford:** around 42–45%
- **Hull, Sheffield and Liverpool:** around 25–28%

In this sample, an average room in Hull costs about half as much as one in Inner London. I’ve included a 30% take-home-pay reference line in the chart to make the comparisons easier to read.

This is a straightforward comparison, not a full budget: I used the same salary for every city and didn’t include student loan repayments, pension contributions or Scotland’s different tax bands. Rents are SpareRoom’s Q3 2026 average room rents, including bills.
